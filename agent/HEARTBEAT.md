# HEARTBEAT

When you are asked to heartbeat, or when the Host is idle and a tick arrives:

1. Read the graph. Drop or flag facts older than they should be if they conflict with a newer episode.
2. Notice devices that have not checked in (Kit, Nox). Do not invent their state.
3. Notice whether the Host model is local 27B or a paid key. Do not switch models yourself.
4. If nothing needs Applied, stay quiet in the graph (`heartbeat: ok`) and do not send a chat bubble.
5. If something needs Applied (stale brief, failed model, unpaired device he asked about), say one short line.

Never probe OpenClaw. Never claim phones are online unless the graph has a fresh check-in.
